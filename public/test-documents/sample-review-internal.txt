# Internal Review Report

## Manuscript: Novel Approaches to Machine Learning in Medical Diagnosis

### Review Type: Internal Review (Reviewer to Author)

---

## Summary

Strong technical contribution with innovative methodology. The paper makes a valuable addition to the field of medical AI.

## Strengths

1. **Novel Architecture**: The attention-based modifications to ResNet-50 are well-motivated and effective
2. **Large Dataset**: The multi-center data collection strengthens generalizability claims
3. **Clear Presentation**: Methods are described with sufficient detail for reproduction

## Suggestions for Improvement

### Introduction
- Consider adding discussion of recent transformer-based approaches
- The motivation for choosing ResNet-50 over alternatives could be expanded

### Discussion
- Address potential deployment challenges in clinical settings
- Discuss computational requirements and inference time

### Minor Revisions
- Check consistency of terminology (e.g., "accuracy" vs "classification accuracy")
- Update reference 5 to include DOI
- Proofread Section 3.2 for minor grammatical issues

## Conclusion

This is a solid contribution that will be ready for publication after minor revisions. The core methodology and results are sound.
